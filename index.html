The 6-week programme

Six weeks.
Real tools.
A marriage that feels like partnership.

Each week builds on the last. You'll leave every session with something concrete — not just insight, but a next step you can actually take.

Held on Google Meet or your preferred platform, one 60-minute session per week, at a time that works across your time zone.

Week one

Who You Are in This Marriage

We start with you — your needs, your patterns, your unspoken expectations. Before we can change the dynamic, we need to understand what you're bringing to it.

Week two

Finding Your Voice

What do you actually need in this relationship? We build the language to express it clearly, calmly, and without losing yourself in the process.

Week three

What the Conflict Is Really About

Arguments are rarely about what they appear to be. We uncover the underlying needs, fears, and patterns driving your most painful dynamics.

Week four

Rebuilding Connection & Trust

Whether trust has been broken or quietly eroded, this week focuses on rebuilding genuine emotional intimacy — the kind that makes a marriage feel like real partnership.

Week five

Your Voice, Your Boundaries, Your Vision

What do you actually want from this marriage? Not what you think you should want — what you genuinely need. We clarify it and give you the language to communicate it.

Week six

Your Relationship Roadmap

Everything comes together — your patterns, your needs, your non-negotiables, and your next steps. A clear written guide for the relationship you want to build.

Frequently Asked Questions

Relationship & marriage coaching — common questions

What is relationship coaching?

Relationship coaching helps you understand relationship patterns, communicate more clearly, identify your needs and boundaries, and decide what you want to do next.

Can I do relationship coaching if my husband doesn't participate?

Yes. She Thrives is a 1:1 programme for women. The work focuses on what you can understand, communicate, change and choose.

Is relationship coaching the same as couples therapy?

No. Relationship coaching and therapy are different services. She Thrives focuses on coaching, reflection, communication, relationship patterns, boundaries and intentional action.

What happens during the six-week programme?

Each week focuses on a different part of the relationship journey, from understanding patterns and communication to conflict, connection, boundaries, vision and a final Relationship Roadmap.

How much does the programme cost?

Foundation is $497, Engine is $797 and Operator is $1,197.

Is the programme online?

Yes. Sessions are private and conducted online.

How do I know which plan is right for me?

The free 30-minute clarity call is designed to help determine whether the programme is appropriate and which level of support best fits your needs.
